One-line roast of a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight — the fridge as a petty landlord, serving a violation notice on its own door.

> My fridge taped a violation notice to its own door at midnight: third offence this week — lease terminated until breakfast.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
